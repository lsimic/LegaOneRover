# LegaOneRover
Source Code and other files related to the LegaOne Rover, developed in the project "3D tvornica buducnosti" for Electrotechnic and traffic school Osijek, Croatia.

LegaOne rover was developed 2015.-2016. by a team of 6 students and their mentor, Mirko Mesic,  from Electrotechnic and traffic school Osijek, Croatia
The main goal of the project "3D tvornica buducnosti/3d factory of the future" was to give students hands on experience and teach them to use new and upcomming technologies
The project is still ongoing, and rover is left for younger students to study and upgrade.
